# Starry Scramble
A constellation-inspired game where the player completes minigames to unlock a constellation's story!


**Play: [Starry Scramble](https://lantyrn.itch.io/starry-scramble)**


<img width="720" height="405" alt="game_preview" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c5c1918-15cc-4720-b879-8ba8d994ad7b" />




## About
Pan around a constellation map and click on stars to discover new constellations. Complete minigames to fill the map with connected constellations and unlock the constellation's story! 
### Currently Unlocked Minigames
1. Ursa Major: Dodge the arrows!
2. Draco: Protect the golden apples!
3. Cygnus: Swim past obstacles and collect stars!
4. Cassiopeia: Precision minigame!
5. Orion: Avoid the scorpion!


### Survival Mode
Play a randomly selected assortment of minigames! With only 5 lives, challenge yourself to see how many minigames you can complete.
